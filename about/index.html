REF: OUR-STORY

A Family Engineering Legacy

Bearma Staal was founded as a family business in Paarl, Western Cape and has grown over more than 25 years into a trusted name in steel engineering and manufacturing.

The business expanded from quality craftsmanship into a full-service engineering workshop, designing and building custom machinery for the food and beverage industry alongside structural steel, security and contracting work throughout the Cape Winelands and greater Western Cape.
